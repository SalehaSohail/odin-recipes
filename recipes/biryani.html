Biryani

Description

Biryani is a popular and flavorful South Asian rice dish made with fragrant basmati rice, tender chicken, aromatic spices, and herbs.

  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 500g chicken
  • 2 onions
  • 2 tomatoes
  • ½ cup yogurt
  • 1 tablespoon ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 tablespoons biryani masala
  • 2 green chilies
  • Fresh coriander and mint
  • Salt to taste
  • Cooking Oil , Water

Steps:

  1. Wash and soak the basmati rice for 20–30 minutes.
  2. Heat oil in a pot and fry the sliced onions until golden brown.
  3. Add ginger-garlic paste and chicken. Cook for a few minutes.
  4. Add tomatoes, yogurt, biryani masala, green chilies, and salt. Mix well.
  5. Cook until the chicken is tender and the gravy thickens.
  6. Boil the rice separately until it is about 70% cooked, then drain the water.
  7. Layer the cooked rice over the chicken gravy.
  8. Sprinkle fresh coriander and mint leaves on top.
  9. Cover the pot and cook on low heat for 15–20 minutes (dum).
  10. Gently mix the biryani and serve hot with raita or salad.
