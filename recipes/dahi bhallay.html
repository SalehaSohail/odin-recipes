Dahi Bhallay

Dahi Bhallay are a popular Pakistani snack made with soft lentil dumplings soaked in creamy yogurt and topped with tangy chutneys and spices.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup gram lentils (chana dal)
  • 1 cup yogurt
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 2 tbsp tamarind chutney
  • 1 tbsp mint chutney
  • 1 tsp chaat masala
  • Salt to taste
  • Oil for frying

Steps:

  1. Soak the lentils for 4–5 hours, then blend them into a thick batter.
  2. Add salt and mix well.
  3. Shape the batter into small balls and deep-fry until golden.
  4. Soak the fried bhallay in warm water for a few minutes, then gently squeeze out the water.
  5. Place them in yogurt and add sugar, salt, chaat masala, and chutneys.
  6. Serve chilled and enjoy!
